generateTokenizerJSONIfMissing implementation in MLX Audio Swift revision
aee9bd1dffcf786f544d6562d971b3e25221e261 (MIT). It was generated exclusively
from vocab.json, merges.txt and tokenizer_config.json in the public model
mlx-community/Qwen3-ASR-0.6B-8bit, revision
89e96d92ba34aca20b3e29fb10cc284097d1219f (Apache-2.0).
The upstream model is Qwen/Qwen3-ASR-0.6B, published by the Qwen team.

This exact tokenizer was used in Roma's native eight-bit accuracy control.
Its SHA256 is a1b84857f2052751736e1ca96e208da455db7de53bea3b6e52c34600d59ec192.
Original input checksums and the weight checksum are in snapshot.json.
Roma installs this derived artifact rather than regenerating dictionary order
at runtime. No model weights are bundled with the application.
